# Demo profile photos

Pictures for the sample members in `src/data/sampleProfiles.ts`.

**Only use images of people who do not exist** (AI-generated faces) that your licence allows
in a dating / matchmaking demo — for example Generated Photos, with the licence in your name.
Never use photos of real people taken from the internet. Every sample member is shown with a
"Demo profile · not a real person" label.

## Current placeholders (testing only)

The `.jpg` files here now are **temporary placeholders** downloaded from thispersondoesnotexist.com
(StyleGAN faces of people who do not exist). Their licence is unclear for anything beyond local
testing, so replace them with properly licensed images before any public or commercial use.
That generator rarely produces Black faces, so most Black members still use illustrations.

## How to add photos

1. Save each image in this folder, named after the member's first name (lowercase), e.g. `adaeze.jpg`.
   `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png` and `.webp` all work.
2. Use a square, head-and-shoulders crop, at least 400 × 400 px, under ~150 KB.
3. Restart `npm run dev` (or rebuild). Members with a file show the photo; the rest keep their illustration.
